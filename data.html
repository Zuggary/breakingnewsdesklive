Not used

  • News articles: never copied. Only links to official pages.
  • WHO Disease Outbreak News and CDC outbreak pages: linked, not read, because of their terms.
  • Smithsonian weekly volcano report: its terms do not allow commercial use.
  • Power outage maps: no open feed.
  • NHTSA vehicle recalls, USDA FSIS recalls, NTSB reports: no stable public feed with clear terms. We may add links later.
  • Space launch schedules: free sources limit requests too tightly for a 10 minute job.
  • Microsoft 365 status is read from our sister project Is it 364 yet, not from Microsoft directly.

How a score is made

Severity gives up to 50 points: the source's own level, or our reading of a magnitude or wind speed. Size gives up to 30. Freshness gives up to 20 and fades over about a day. Repeated US warnings of one kind lose 10 points each. The score only orders the list. It says nothing about danger to you.
