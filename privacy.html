Privacy

This site has no accounts, no cookies and no ads, and it counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies (see Visit counts). We do not collect personal data.

What your browser stores

This site sets no cookies, shows no ads, and has no accounts. The site caches its own files on your device so it works offline; nothing you type is stored or sent. You can clear the cache in your browser settings.

Visit counts

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits. It sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint visitors. It sees the page address without anything after a question mark, the site you came from, your country, your browser, your operating system, your device type and how fast the page loaded. To make the request, your browser shows Cloudflare your IP address, as it does to any web server. It does not see anything you type. When you click an e-mail link, the page also sends Cloudflare one fixed word for that click, such as out-mail. It never sends the link address. We see only totals. We cannot see which visitor did what, and nothing follows you from one visit to the next.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement. The only script it loads from another site is the Cloudflare visit counter (see Visit counts). It loads no fonts from other sites.

Images from other sites

Some event cards show a photo or a satellite view. Your browser loads these images only when you open a card. The photo comes from Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org). The satellite view comes from NASA Worldview (wvs.earthdata.nasa.gov). Those sites can see your IP address and the page you came from, as with any image on any site. We do not send them anything else. Wikipedia text is fetched by our data job, not by your browser.

Links

Event links go to government and agency sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
