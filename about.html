About

Breaking News Desk is an independent, automated project. It shows facts as they come in from official and public sources: natural hazards, air travel disruption, service outages and more. A script collects the data about every 10 minutes. No person reviews each item before it appears.

World events

World events items are claims by a named party or outlet. We show "Party says" statements from official feeds and the headlines of outlets as links. We do not copy articles, we do not verify the claims, and we show no outlet text beyond its headline. Headlines come from the GDELT Project, grouped by similar wording. The grouping can be wrong.

Our rules

  1. Facts only. We say what happened, where, when, and who reported it. No opinion, no blame, no prediction.
  2. Primary sources first. Every item names its source and the time the source reported it.
  3. Plain words and numbers as the source gives them.
  4. Same rules for every topic. No topic ranks higher because of who it affects politically.
  5. Neutral ranking by size, severity, people or flights affected, and freshness. Never by outrage or clicks.
  6. We show uncertainty and correct fast.
  7. No emergency instructions. We link to the official agency.
  8. No names of private people.

Check official sources. This site is not an emergency service. In an emergency, follow your local authorities.

How items are ranked

The ranking rule is public and the same for every item.

Each event gets a score from 0 to 100. The score adds severity (up to 50), size (up to 30) and freshness (up to 20, fading over about a day). Routine US weather warnings are capped so they do not push out large global events. The summary line is built from a fixed template and uses only values from the source data.

Report an error or ask for a correction

Corrections are public. When we fix an item, we say what changed and when.

Write to contact@breakingnewsdesklive.com. Include the event title and the time shown. For the fastest fix, tell the source agency too.
