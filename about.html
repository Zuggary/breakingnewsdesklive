About

Breaking News Desk shows facts as they come in from official and public sources. It covers natural hazards, air travel disruption, service outages, recalls, cyber and official notices, and space weather. A script collects the data about every 10 minutes. No person checks each routine item before it appears. A person reviews items held by the crisis switch.

It is an independent, automated project. It is not a news organization, and no agency is affiliated with it.

Check official sources. This site is not an emergency service. In an emergency, follow your local authorities.

Our rules

  1. Facts only. We say what happened, where, when, and who reported it. No opinion, no blame, no prediction.
  2. Primary sources first. Every item names its source and the time the source reported it.
  3. Plain words, and numbers as the source gives them.
  4. The same rules for every topic. No topic ranks higher because of who it affects politically.
  5. Neutral ranking by size, severity, people or flights affected, and freshness. Never by outrage or clicks.
  6. We show uncertainty and correct fast.
  7. No emergency instructions. We link to the official agency.
  8. No names of private people.

How items are ranked

Each item gets a score from 0 to 100. Severity gives up to 50 points, size up to 30, and freshness up to 20, fading over about a day. Repeated US warnings of one kind lose points, so they do not fill the list. The score only orders the list. It says nothing about danger to you.

World events

World events items are claims by a named party or outlet. Official statements show as "Party: title" with a link. Outlet headlines come from the GDELT Project and show as the outlet's own headline with a link. We do not copy articles, and we do not check the claims. The grouping of headlines can be wrong. World events are not shown on the stream.

Masthead and safety checks

Breaking News Desk is an independent, automated project with one human owner. Contact: contact@breakingnewsdesklive.com. To report a mistake, use the corrections page.

  • This is an AI-assisted wire desk. Software collects the feeds and writes short summaries from fixed templates. No language model rewrites source text.
  • A check blocks any summary that holds a number or a name that is not in the source data. A second check blocks blame, suspect and casualty words in our own text.
  • Claims by outlets or parties carry the label UNVERIFIED. New items under 90 minutes old carry the label DEVELOPING.
  • A crisis switch holds items about mass casualties, crashes, shootings, attacks and war until a person clears them. A person can also stop all publishing.
  • Generated pictures carry the tag Illustration. They never show a real scene.

Corrections

We fix errors fast and say what changed. See the corrections page to report one and to read past corrections. Email: contact@breakingnewsdesklive.com.
